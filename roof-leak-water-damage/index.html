Emergency Roof Leak Water Damage in American Fork, UT

Water that comes in through the roof ends up in the attic, the insulation and the ceilings below. We handle what is wet inside the house. A roofer handles the roof.

Call (385) 988-9410

A roof leak rarely shows up where the roof is damaged. Water enters at a worn shingle, a lifted flashing or a cracked vent boot, runs along the decking or a rafter, and soaks insulation before it stains a ceiling. By the time you see a mark, the attic may already be wet. This page covers the roof and attic path only. For a whole-event storm response, see storm water damage.

How roofs let water in around American Fork

NOAA 1991 to 2020 climate normals at the Pleasant Grove station, next to American Fork, give about 41.4 inches of snowfall per year. The same station averages about 18.5 days per year with at least an inch of snow. That is a lot of freezing, melting and refreezing on a roof.

Common entry points follow that pattern.

  • Ice at the eaves that holds meltwater behind it so it backs up under shingles.
  • Flashing around chimneys, skylights and walls that has lifted or cracked.
  • Rubber vent boots around plumbing stacks that split with age.
  • Shingles damaged by hail or lifted by wind.
  • Nail pops and exposed fasteners in older roofs.

A roofer is the right trade for tarping, shingle repair and flashing. We do not repair roofs, and we say so plainly. What we do is dry and restore everything that got wet inside.

A stain that grows only after snow melts points toward ice at the eaves or flashing. One that grows during rain suggests a vent boot or a storm-damaged section of roof.

First-hour safety steps

  1. Stay clear of a sagging ceiling

    If a ceiling is bulging, sagging or dripping heavily, leave the room and do not stand under it. Water can pool in drywall until it lets go all at once.

  2. Leave the release to us

    Do not poke a hole in a bulging ceiling yourself. Water and wet drywall drop on whoever is standing below, so we release it in a controlled way.

  3. Watch the electricity

    If water drips at a light fixture, switch the fixture off from a dry position, such as at a wall switch, and leave the breaker alone if you would stand in water to reach it.

  4. Stay off the roof

    A snowy or wet roof is slippery. Do not climb up to look. Call a roofer to tarp and repair it.

  5. Protect and document

    Move furniture and rugs out of rooms that are safe to enter, set out containers and photograph the stains before cleanup.

How we handle a wet ceiling and attic

We use ANSI/IICRC S500, the standard for professional water damage restoration, as our reference. The work starts with moisture readings and a thermal camera sweep to find how far the water spread.

  • Controlled release of water trapped in a bulging ceiling, so it does not fall on its own.
  • Removal of wet ceiling drywall and insulation that cannot be dried.
  • Drying of the attic and wall cavities with air movers and dehumidifiers.
  • Containment with plastic sheeting so dust and insulation stay out of living space.
  • Repeated moisture readings until framing is dry.

The EPA says to dry water-damaged areas within 24 to 48 hours to prevent mold growth, which is why a ceiling leak found late is urgent. If the water has gone through to a wall or floor, see water damage cleanup. We also serve Lehi and Alpine.

Where insurance fits

Policies differ on sudden storm damage and long-term wear. The Utah Insurance Department tells policyholders to call their insurer promptly, take photographs and make temporary repairs to prevent further damage. Keep receipts for tarps and cleanup, ask your insurer what applies and avoid permanent repairs until an inspection has happened.
