Find and stop the source

Close the fixture valve or the main shutoff. If the water comes from the secondary irrigation system, close your service valve and tell the city.

Power first, then people

Switch off wet circuits only if you can reach the panel dry. Keep children and pets out of any water that came from a drain.

Lift and photograph

Raise furniture onto blocks, pull up small rugs and photograph every wet surface before you move things.

The water problems Pleasant Grove homes see most

The city says a broken pipe in its secondary system is likely caused by freezing over the winter. A cracked irrigation line sits quietly until the city starts filling the system around mid-April, and then it can soak a yard, a window well or a basement wall. If you find damp carpet along an exterior wall in spring, a hidden irrigation break is a reasonable suspect, and the water is untreated secondary water.

Foothill runoff is the second theme.

Battle Creek and Grove Creek both come down from the west face of Mount Timpanogos toward town, and the city lists trailheads for each on its east side, at 1700 East 200 South and 1600 East 500 North. Homes in that part of town sit closest to snowmelt channels in a heavy runoff year. Check the FEMA flood map for your address and ask your insurer about flood coverage, which standard homeowners policies typically exclude.
