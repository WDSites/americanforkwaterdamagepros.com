Water Damage Restoration in AF Crossings, American Fork, UT

On lots with lawns, sprinklers and irrigation lines, water often arrives from outside. Here is what to do first in an AF Crossings home and how drying works.

Call (385) 988-9410

AF Crossings is a newer community in the southwest part of American Fork. City planning notices list an AF Crossings plat at about 856 South 1000 West. A listing-site summary reports that all sampled homes were built in 2020 or later, with a median lot of about 0.17 acre. A national builder also markets a community called American Fork Crossing, and we have not confirmed that it matches the city's plat name.

We cannot say what any one house has. What we can say is that homes on lots of that size have yards, irrigation and exterior plumbing that smaller attached homes do not.

How yard and irrigation water becomes interior damage

  • The city's pressurized irrigation system runs from about mid-April to mid-October. A broken sprinkler line can saturate soil next to a foundation.
  • A hose bib or exterior line that fails can send water into a wall or lower level.
  • Window wells and low spots can collect runoff during heavy rain or snowmelt.
  • Water service lines must be buried at least 48 inches deep according to city standards, so damage there is usually outside the home.

Our water removal page explains how we extract water that has already entered. For storm water, see storm water damage.

Safety in the first hour

Do these things first

  • ✓Shut off irrigation at the controller if sprinklers are the source.
  • ✓Close the main house valve if a supply line is leaking inside.
  • ✓Do not step into water in a lower level if outlets or cords are submerged.
  • ✓Keep children and pets out of wet rooms.
  • ✓Photograph the source, damage and any outdoor ponding.

Inside the house

In a newer two-story home, water in a lower level or unfinished area can wick into framing, drywall and carpet. Our wet carpet drying page explains how pad and carpet are handled.

How drying is planned

  1. Find the water path

    Meters show whether water came from the roof, a pipe, a window well or the ground.

  2. Extract

    Standing water is pumped or extracted first.

  3. Dry

    Air movers and dehumidifiers run until readings reach dry targets, following ANSI/IICRC S500.

  4. Document

    Readings and photos are kept for your records.
