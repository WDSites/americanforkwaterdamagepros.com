Newer homes, different failure points

The city's General Plan calls Lehi one of the fastest growing communities in the Western United States. In a house that is only a few years old, a leak rarely comes from corroded pipe. A washing machine hose, a water heater connection or a refrigerator line is the more likely culprit, and a finished basement can hide the result for weeks. Moisture meters and thermal imaging find what the eye cannot.

In older parts of the city, the story shifts. Wines Park, at 500 to 600 North Center, and the Lehi Roller Mills at 900 East Main from 1905 and 1906 sit in a part of town where homes were built decades before the subdivisions. Older supply lines and cast iron drains deserve a plumber's attention before they fail.

Lehi at a glance

ZIP 84043. The city Water Department runs culinary water, sewer and pressurized irrigation. Landmarks include Wines Park, Thanksgiving Point, the Main Street core and Utah Lake to the south. Our dispatch line is available around the clock for emergencies.

Dry Creek, the Murdock Canal and Lehi's drainage

Lehi's storm drain plan calls Dry Creek the only FEMA-studied channel in the city and traces it from the northeast through the middle of town to Utah Lake.

Other drainages include the Waste Ditch, which flows to the Jordan River, Cedar Hollow, Spring Creek and the Murdock Canal along SR-92. The canal was piped in 2012. Homes near any of these should know whether they sit inside a mapped floodplain, because the city's floodplain rules appear in its Development Code and work in the 100-year floodplain needs a floodplain development permit.

The city is a National Flood Insurance Program member, which means residents can buy flood coverage. A standard flood policy typically takes 30 days to become active, so the time to buy is well before spring. Standard homeowners coverage typically excludes flood.

When water is spreading in Lehi

If water is still moving through your Lehi home, the first minutes matter. Our 24/7 line takes the call at any hour, and we offer same-day response when available. Do these things while the crew is arranged.

Shut it off

Close the main water valve at the house, or the shutoff under the fixture. A water heater leak has its own shutoff on the supply line.

Stay out of live water

If it touches outlets or the panel, stay out and call the utility or 911 first. Do not unplug anything while standing in water.

Move what you can

Take electronics, papers and soft furniture to a dry room, and photograph the damage before moving it.

Call with your address

Tell dispatch your street, the source and how long the water has been there so the right equipment is loaded.

Permits, cost and insurance
